Kluay

A light with your name on it

Touching the brass bar

Everything is done with the metal bar along the front. Rest a finger on it — no pressing needed.

  • Tap Change the light Three to choose from. If it's off, a tap wakes it.
  • Hold 1s Brighter or softer Two levels. The light pauses a moment so you can see the change.
  • Hold 3s Turn it off It fades out while your finger is still there.
  • Hold 10s Open Wi-Fi setup Only needed once. See below.

Days it knows

On these dates the light becomes something else. It returns to normal the next day.

  • 24 DecemberThe day we began
  • 6 NovemberYour birthday
  • 3 MarchThe day we met
  • The 24thEvery month
I'm not telling you what happens. You'll have to wait and see. This is why it needs Wi-Fi — without it, the light can't know what day it is, and those days will pass like any other.

Connecting to Wi-Fi

Once. After this it reconnects on its own, every time you plug it in.

  1. Hold the brass bar for 10 seconds. The light fades out at 3 seconds — keep holding. At 10 it starts pulsing white.
  2. Open Wi-Fi on your phone and join KluayLight. No password.
  3. A setup page should open by itself. If it doesn't, go to 192.168.4.1 in your browser. Your phone will warn you there's no internet on this network. That's expected — stay connected.
  4. Tap Configure WiFi, choose your home network, enter its password, save.
  5. The white pulsing stops and the light comes back to normal. Done. Your router needs 2.4GHz. Most have it alongside 5GHz.

If it blinks white

White is the only colour it uses to tell you something.

Three quick blinks when you plug it in, or when you wake it — it can't reach your Wi-Fi. Everything still works; it just won't know the date. Run the setup again.
Slow steady pulsing — setup is open and waiting for your phone. Hold the bar 3 seconds to close it.
The white stops and the light comes back — connected.

The paper

Mino washi, made in Gifu — one of the places Japan has been making paper by hand for over a thousand years.

The fine threads you can see when it's lit are barley stalks, laid into the sheet while it was still wet. No two pieces are the same. I chose this one out of everything the shop had.

Looking after it

  • Keep it out of direct sun. The case and the letters soften in real heat.
  • The front is washi paper. Don't wipe it — dust the acrylic instead.
  • Leave it plugged in. It uses almost nothing, and turning it off with the bar is easier than the cable.
  • Any 5V USB charger works. A phone charger is fine.
